## 1. Executive Summary & Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Synapse · ContextRelay ("Synapse", "the extension", "we", "our") is designed from the ground up on a **Zero-Knowledge, 100% Client-Side Architecture**. 

- **No Developer Servers:** We do not own, operate, or maintain any intermediary backend servers, proxy relays, database servers, or collection endpoints.
- **No Analytics or Telemetry:** Synapse contains zero analytics packages, tracking pixels, crash-reporting daemons, fingerprinting scripts, or telemetry beacons.
- **Direct Client-to-Provider Communication:** All network requests containing prompts or context originate directly from your local browser client and travel straight to the official API endpoint of your selected AI model provider (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or Groq) using your own personal API keys.
- **Your Data Remains Yours:** The developer never receives, sees, stores, logs, or monetizes your prompts, responses, browsing history, webpage content, or credentials.

## 2. API Key Security & Cryptographic Pipeline

Synapse operates on a strict **Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK)** model. Because API keys grant access to billable cloud resources, Synapse treats key security with military-grade zero-knowledge cryptography:

### 2.1. Master PIN & Key Derivation (PBKDF2)
- When you initialize Synapse, you select a personal numeric **Master PIN**.
- Synapse never saves your PIN in plaintext anywhere.
- Your PIN derives a high-entropy 256-bit encryption key using the standard Web Crypto API (`PBKDF2`) configured with **600,000 iterations of SHA-256** and a 16-byte cryptographically secure random salt generated via `crypto.getRandomValues()`.

### 2.2. Authenticated Encryption (AES-GCM 256-bit)
- Your provider API keys are encrypted locally using **256-bit AES-GCM** (Galois/Counter Mode), providing both strong confidentiality and cryptographic ciphertext integrity.
- Each encryption operation generates a unique, single-use 12-byte Initialization Vector (IV).
- The resulting Base64-encoded ciphertext, salt, and IV are stored in your browser's persistent `chrome.storage.local`. Without your Master PIN, the vault payload is unreadable noise.

### 2.3. Ephemeral In-Memory Session Storage (`chrome.storage.session`)
- When you unlock Synapse with your Master PIN, decrypted API keys are held exclusively inside **`chrome.storage.session`**.
- `chrome.storage.session` is an in-memory storage area managed by the Chromium browser runtime. Decrypted keys **never touch your local disk**, are never committed to permanent storage, and are wiped automatically whenever your browser session closes or when you click the Lock button.
- Plaintext API keys are never printed to console logs or shared across extension boundaries.

### 2.4. Brute-Force Rate Limiting
- To protect your Master PIN against automated brute-force attacks on your physical device, Synapse enforces a lockout counter in local storage. Entering 5 consecutive incorrect PINs immediately triggers an irreversible 30-second lockout timer, preventing repeated verification attempts even if the popup is closed and reopened.

## 3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We hold a zero-data collection policy. Specifically, the extension does **NOT** collect, log, transmit, or share:

1. **Personally Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or device IDs.
2. **Browsing Activity & History:** We do not track websites visited, browsing durations, click events, search queries, or referrer headers.
3. **Webpage Content & DOM Data:** Page text is never transmitted to us. Extracted context is held temporarily in browser memory solely to construct the prompt payload requested by you.
4. **AI Conversations & Model Outputs:** Neither your prompts nor the streamed responses generated by language models are ever recorded by or visible to the extension developer.
5. **Cookies & Credentials:** Synapse does not access web cookies, session tokens, passwords, or autofill credentials on any webpage.

---

## 4. On-Device PII Scrubbing & Context Hygiene

Before any extracted webpage text is sent to an AI provider:
- Synapse runs an automated, client-side regex scrubber (`scrubPII`) directly in browser memory.
- The scrubber redacts email addresses, IPv4/IPv6 addresses, bearer tokens, and standard cloud/API keys (`sk-...`, `gsk_...`, `AIza...`), replacing them with `[REDACTED]`.
- Synapse also calculates character token estimates (`Math.ceil(chars / 4)`) and automatically prunes bloated conversation histories that exceed 80% of model limits, keeping your system prompt and the 3 most recent conversation turns intact.

## 5. Extension Permissions & Justifications

In compliance with the Chrome Web Store Minimum Permissions Policy, Synapse requests only the minimal set of browser permissions strictly required to execute its core user-directed functions:

| Permission | Technical Requirement | User-Facing Purpose & Safeguards |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`"tabs"`** | Access `tab.url` and query active tab metadata. | Used strictly on-demand when you click "Relay Context" to inspect the active tab's URL. This ensures Synapse immediately suppresses DOM extraction on sensitive browser internal pages (`chrome://`, `chrome-extension://`, `about:`, `chromewebstore...`) and displays an inline safety warning banner. |
| **`"scripting"`** | Programmatic content script execution via `chrome.scripting.executeScript`. | Injects the context extractor into the active webpage **only on-demand** when you explicitly press the "Relay Context" button. Synapse does not run background content scripts continuously on every page load. |
| **`"activeTab"`** | Contextual temporary tab delegation. | Grants temporary, elevated tab access during active user interaction, ensuring script injection operates safely within standard user-gesture lifecycles. |
| **`"storage"`** | Access `chrome.storage.local` and `chrome.storage.session`. | `chrome.storage.local` is used solely to store the encrypted key vault and non-sensitive UI preferences (such as your last selected model). `chrome.storage.session` holds in-memory unlocked keys and active streaming buffers that vanish when Chrome closes. |
| **`host_permissions`**<br>`https://*/*`<br>`http://*/*` | Outbound network calls and script injection. | Required for two specific functions:<br>1. Allows injecting the content extractor into standard user-selected web pages upon your explicit command.<br>2. Enables direct, browser-to-cloud HTTPS fetch calls to official AI model API endpoints (`api.openai.com`, `api.anthropic.com`, `generativelanguage.googleapis.com`, `api.groq.com`). |

## 6. Third-Party AI Model Providers

When you trigger a context relay, Synapse sends your scrubbed context directly to the AI service provider corresponding to your chosen model:

- **OpenAI:** [https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)
- **Anthropic:** [https://www.anthropic.com/privacy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy)
- **Google Gemini:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Groq:** [https://groq.com/privacy-policy/](https://groq.com/privacy-policy/)
- **Custom Provider Endpoints:** If you configure custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints, requests are sent strictly to the URL you specify.

Data processed by these third-party AI providers is governed exclusively by your direct customer agreement and their respective privacy policies. Synapse acts purely as a local client-side bridge.

## 7. Data Retention & User Controls

You maintain total control over your local data at all times:
- **Immediate Lock:** Clicking the Lock icon in the popup header immediately purges decrypted keys from `chrome.storage.session`.
- **Vault Erase / Reconfiguration:** You can modify or overwrite your stored API keys at any time through the Settings drawer.
- **Total Uninstall:** Removing Synapse from Chrome (`chrome://extensions` → Remove) immediately and permanently deletes all stored encrypted vaults, settings, and cached states from your device.
